Game Rules · 游戏规则

怎么玩？

Party mode: one person reads the question aloud, everyone else calls out 是！ or 不是！ — then tap the card to reveal the answer. Surprise answers win extra points. Play in teams or alone!
是！YES — it really is.
不是！NO — it's not what it looks like.
是……吗？Is it a …?
揭秘reveal the answer
The trick: many Chinese words contain a smaller word inside — but that doesn't mean they're related. 蜗牛 (snail) contains 牛 (cow) — but a snail is NOT a cow! Sometimes though, the answer IS yes… and that's the surprise. 🎉

Round 4 · 食物大挑战 · Food Challenge

这些食物是它们名字里的东西吗？

🎯 0/4 猜对了
🌭
热狗是狗吗？
tap to reveal →
不是！
热狗 = hot dog. No actual dogs! 🐶 It's a sausage (香肠) — borrowed from the English "hot dog." Name-only coincidence.
🍸
鸡尾酒是酒吗？
tap to reveal →
是！🎉
鸡尾酒 = cocktail. YES — it IS alcohol (含酒精)! Translated as "rooster-tail liquor." Definitely 酒 (alcoholic drink).
🍖
火腿是腿吗？
tap to reveal →
是！🎉
火腿 = ham. YES — it literally IS a leg (后腿 hind leg of a pig)! 腿 means leg. Cured pig leg = ham. Perfectly named!
🧊
豆腐是腐的吗？
tap to reveal →
不是！
豆腐 = tofu. 腐 (fǔ) means "rotten/fermented" — but fresh tofu isn't rotten! The word refers to a curd/transformation process, not rot. (臭豆腐 IS, though 😅)

Round 5 · 植物和自然 · Plants & Nature

这些植物是它们名字里的东西吗？

🎯 0/4 猜对了
🌽
玉米是米吗？
tap to reveal →
不是！
玉米 = corn (maize). 米 usually means rice or a rice-like grain. Corn is a cereal too, but a completely different plant (禾本科).
🥜
花生是花吗？
tap to reveal →
不是！
花生 = peanut. 花 means flower, but peanuts aren't flowers! The name means "flower-born" — after pollination the stem buries itself and the nut grows underground.
🍓
草莓是草吗？
tap to reveal →
不是！
草莓 = strawberry. 草 means grass, but strawberries aren't grass (though the plant is low to the ground like grass). Delicious confusion! 🍓
🫐
无花果真的没有花吗？
tap to reveal →
有花！🎉
无花果 = fig. The name means "no-flower fruit" — but figs DO have flowers! They're hidden INSIDE the fruit. The flower blooms inward. Sneaky! 🌸
