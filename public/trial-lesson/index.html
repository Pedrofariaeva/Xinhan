How It Really Works · 手机点单 (1/3)

大部分时候，你是用手机点单的 Dà bùfen shíhou, nǐ shì yòng shǒujī diǎndān de.

Reality check: at Luckin (and most chains in China) you rarely speak to staff at all — you order in the app or a mini-program, and a screen number just calls you when it's ready. These four screens are what you actually see before you even pick a drink.

选好以后：规格、付钱、取餐 Xuǎn hǎo yǐhòu: guīgé, fùqián, qǔcān.
