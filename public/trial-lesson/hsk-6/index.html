grammar
Grammar · 语法

Key sentence patterns guānjiàn jùxíng

随着…，…
along with…, …
随着科技的发展，人们的生活方式发生了很大的变化。
Suízhe kējì de fāzhǎn, rénmen de shēnghuó fāngshì fāshēng le hěn dà de biànhuà.
Along with the development of technology, people's lifestyles have changed greatly.
不仅…，而且…
not only…, but also…
人工智能不仅提高了效率，而且改变了我们的工作方式。
Réngōng zhìnéng bùjǐn tígāo le xiàolǜ, érqiě gǎibiàn le wǒmen de gōngzuò fāngshì.
AI not only improves efficiency, but also changes the way we work.
科技的发展对社会有什么影响？
Kējì de fāzhǎn duì shèhuì yǒu shénme yǐngxiǎng?
What impact does technological development have on society?
随着互联网的普及，人们的交流方式发生了巨大变化。
Suízhe hùliánwǎng de pǔjí, rénmen de jiāoliú fāngshì fāshēng le jùdà biànhuà.
With the popularization of the Internet, people's ways of communicating have changed tremendously.
我们不能忽视隐私保护的重要性。
Wǒmen bù néng hūshì yǐnsī bǎohù de zhòngyào xìng.
We cannot ignore the importance of privacy protection.
未来的趋势不仅是技术进步，而且是社会责任的提升。
Wèilái de qūshì bùjǐn shì jìshù jìnbù, érqiě shì shèhuì zérèn de tíshēng.
The future trend is not only technological progress, but also the improvement of social responsibility.
