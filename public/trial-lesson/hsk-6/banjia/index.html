G1 · 倘若
Grammar 1 · 语法1
倘若
tǎng ruò
+
假设
hypothesis
，
结果
result
if / supposing…, then…
倘若房价继续上涨，年轻人买房会更困难。
Tǎng ruò fáng jià jì xù shàng zhǎng, nián qīng rén mǎi fáng huì gèng kùn nan.
If housing prices continue to rise, it will be harder for young people to buy homes.
倘若你提前一个月搬家，就能省不少钱。
Tǎng ruò nǐ tí qián yī gè yuè bān jiā, jiù néng shěng bù shǎo qián.
If you move a month earlier, you can save quite a bit of money.
倘若没有更好的选择，我们不妨先租下来。
Tǎng ruò méi yǒu gèng hǎo de xuǎn zé, wǒ men bù fáng xiān zū xià lái.
If there is no better choice, we might as well rent it first.
倘若 is a formal, literary way to say "if".
G2 · 固然…但
Grammar 2 · 语法2
固然
gù rán
+
承认的事实
acknowledged fact
，
但
dàn
+
转折
contrast
admittedly…, but…
大城市固然机会多，但生活成本也高。
Dà chéng shì gù rán jī huì duō, dàn shēng huó chéng běn yě gāo.
Big cities admittedly offer many opportunities, but the cost of living is also high.
这套房子固然宽敞，但采光不太好。
Zhè tào fáng zi gù rán kuān chǎng, dàn cǎi guāng bù tài hǎo.
This apartment is admittedly spacious, but the lighting is not very good.
搬家固然麻烦，但也是新生活的开始。
Bān jiā gù rán má fan, dàn yě shì xīn shēng huó de kāi shǐ.
Moving is admittedly troublesome, but it is also the start of a new life.
固然…但 acknowledges one side before presenting a contrast.

Grammar 3 · 语法3

与其抱怨房子小，不如努力赚钱换大房。
Yǔ qí bào yuàn fáng zi xiǎo, bù rú nǔ lì zhuàn qián huàn dà fáng.
Rather than complain the house is small, you might as well work hard to afford a bigger one.
与其临时找房，不如提前三个月准备。
Yǔ qí lín shí zhǎo fáng, bù rú tí qián sān gè yuè zhǔn bèi.
Rather than look for a house at the last minute, prepare three months in advance.
与其住豪宅但每天通勤三小时，不如住得近一点。
Yǔ qí zhù háo zhái dàn měi tiān tōng qín sān xiǎo shí, bù rú zhù de jìn yī diǎn.
Rather than live in a mansion but commute three hours a day, you might as well live closer.
与其…不如 suggests a better choice between two options.
dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

买房还是租房？ Mǎi fáng hái shì zū fáng?

A：你打算买新房还是租房？
Nǐ dǎ suàn mǎi xīn fáng hái shì zū fáng?
Do you plan to buy a new house or rent?
B：权衡利弊之后，我决定先租房。倘若以后收入稳定，再考虑买房。
Quán héng lì bì zhī hòu, wǒ jué dìng xiān zū fáng. Tǎng ruò yǐ hòu shōu rù wěn dìng, zài kǎo lǜ mǎi fáng.
After weighing the pros and cons, I’ve decided to rent first. If my income stabilizes later, I’ll consider buying.
A：你不喜欢大房子吗？
Nǐ bù xǐ huan dà fáng zi ma?
Don’t you like big houses?
B：豪宅固然令人羡慕，但维护成本高。与其背负沉重房贷，不如量力而行。
Háo zhái gù rán lìng rén xiàn mù, dàn wéi hù chéng běn gāo. Yǔ qí bēi fù chén zhòng fáng dài, bù rú liàng lì ér xíng.
Mansions are admittedly enviable, but maintenance costs are high. Rather than shoulder a heavy mortgage, you might as well live within your means.
A：这次搬家你准备得怎么样？
Zhè cì bān jiā nǐ zhǔn bèi de zěn me yàng?
How is your preparation for this move?
B：我未雨绸缪，提前收拾好了行李。希望乔迁之后能安居乐业。
Wǒ wèi yǔ chóu móu, tí qián shōu shi hǎo le xíng li. Xī wàng qiáo qiān zhī hòu néng ān jū lè yè.
I prepared in advance and packed my luggage early. I hope after moving I can live and work in peace.
