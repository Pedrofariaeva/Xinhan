G1 · 不言而喻
Grammar 1 · 语法1
不言而喻
bù yán ér yù
self-evident / it goes without saying
好的表达能让人印象深刻，这不言而喻。
Hǎo de biǎodá néng ràng rén yìnxiàng shēnkè, zhè bù yán ér yù.
Good expression leaves a deep impression — it goes without saying.
天气影响心情，不言而喻。
Tiānqì yǐngxiǎng xīnqíng, bù yán ér yù.
Weather affects mood — it goes without saying.
G2 · 各有千秋
Grammar 2 · 语法2
各有千秋
gè yǒu qiān qiū
Each has its own merits
直白的表达和含蓄的表达各有千秋。
Zhíbái de biǎodá hé hánxù de biǎodá gè yǒu qiān qiū.
Direct and implicit expressions each have their own merits.
口语和书面语各有千秋。
Kǒuyǔ hé shūmiànyǔ gè yǒu qiān qiū.
Spoken and written language each have their own merits.
G3 · 见仁见智
Grammar 3 · 语法3
见仁见智
jiàn rén jiàn zhì
Opinions differ
哪种说法最好，见仁见智。
Nǎ zhǒng shuōfǎ zuì hǎo, jiàn rén jiàn zhì.
Which way of saying it is best — opinions differ.
对这个观点，大家见仁见智。
Duì zhège guāndiǎn, dàjiā jiàn rén jiàn zhì.
Everyone has different opinions on this viewpoint.
dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

文学化的表达 Wénxué huà de biǎodá

A — Ask
你觉得口语表达和书面表达最大的区别是什么？
Nǐ juéde kǒuyǔ biǎodá hé shūmiàn biǎodá zuì dà de qūbié shì shénme?
What do you think is the biggest difference between spoken and written expression?
B — Answer
各有千秋。口语更直接，书面语更有韵味和层次。
Gè yǒu qiān qiū. Kǒuyǔ gèng zhíjiē, shūmiànyǔ gèng yǒu yùnwèi hé céngcì.
Each has its merits. Spoken is more direct; written has more charm and depth.
A — Ask
那用修辞是不是一定更好？
Nà yòng xiūcí shì bùshì yídìng gèng hǎo?
Then is using rhetoric always better?
B — Answer
见仁见智。恰当才有内涵，过度反而显得夸张。
Jiàn rén jiàn zhì. Qiàdàng cái yǒu nèihán, guòdù fǎn'ér xiǎnde kuāzhāng.
Opinions differ. Only when appropriate does it have connotation; overuse appears exaggerated.
