G1 · 之所以…是因为
Grammar 1 · 语法1
之所以
zhī suǒ yǐ
+
结果
result
，
是因为
shì yīn wèi
+
原因
reason
the reason why… is because…
我之所以搬家，是因为工作调动。
Wǒ zhī suǒ yǐ bān jiā, shì yīn wèi gōng zuò diào dòng.
The reason I moved house is because of a job transfer.
他之所以选择这个小区，是因为设施完善。
Tā zhī suǒ yǐ xuǎn zé zhè ge xiǎo qū, shì yīn wèi shè shī wán shàn.
The reason he chose this community is because the facilities are complete.
我们之所以提前搬家，是因为孩子要上学。
Wǒ men zhī suǒ yǐ tí qián bān jiā, shì yīn wèi hái zi yào shàng xué.
The reason we moved earlier is because the child needs to go to school.
之所以…是因为 explains the reason for a result.
G2 · 随着
Grammar 2 · 语法2
随着
suí zhe
+
变化

，
结果
result
along with…, …
随着城市的发展，房价越来越高。
Suí zhe chéng shì de fā zhǎn, fáng jià yuè lái yuè gāo.
Along with the city’s development, housing prices keep rising.
随着工作的变动，我不得不搬家。
Suí zhe gōng zuò de biàn dòng, wǒ bù dé bù bān jiā.
Along with the job change, I had to move house.
随着年龄的增长，人们更看重居住品质。
Suí zhe nián líng de zēng zhǎng, rén men gèng kàn zhòng jū zhù pǐn zhì.
As people age, they value living quality more.
随着 introduces a background change that leads to a result.

既然决定了，就早点行动。
Jì rán jué dìng le, jiù zǎo diǎn xíng dòng.

既然房子已经找到了，就开始收拾吧。
Jì rán fáng zi yǐ jīng zhǎo dào le, jiù kāi shǐ shōu shi ba.
Since the house has already been found, start packing.
既然预算有限，就减少不必要的开支。

Since the budget is limited, reduce unnecessary expenses.
既然…就 accepts a fact and draws a conclusion.
dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

你又要搬家了？ Nǐ yòu yào bān jiā le?

A：听说你又要搬家了？
Tīng shuō nǐ yòu yào bān jiā le?
I heard you are moving again?
B：是啊，随着公司搬迁到新区，我也必须跟着搬。
Shì a, suí zhe gōng sī bān qiān dào xīn qū, wǒ yě bì xū gēn zhe bān.
Yes. As the company relocates to the new district, I have to move too.
A：你之所以选那个地方，是因为工作方便吗？
Nǐ zhī suǒ yǐ xuǎn nà ge dì fāng, shì yīn wèi gōng zuò fāng biàn ma?
The reason you chose that place is because it is convenient for work?
B：这只是原因之一。既然要长期居住，我就想找个环境更好、设施更全的小区。
Zhè zhǐ shì yuán yīn zhī yī. Jì rán yào cháng qī jū zhù, wǒ jiù xiǎng zhǎo ge huán jìng gèng hǎo, shè shī gèng quán de xiǎo qū.
That’s only one reason. Since I’m going to live there long-term, I want a community with a better environment and more complete facilities.
A：预算够吗？
Yù suàn gòu ma?
Is your budget enough?
B：虽然预算有限，但权衡之后，我觉得值得。
Suī rán yù suàn yǒu xiàn, dàn quán héng zhī hòu, wǒ jué de zhí de.
Although the budget is limited, after weighing things up, I think it is worth it.
