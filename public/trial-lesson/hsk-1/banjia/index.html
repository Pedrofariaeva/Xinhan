G1 · 是
Grammar 1 · 语法1
A
subject
+
是
shì
+
B
noun
A is B
这是我家。
Zhè shì wǒ jiā.
This is my home.
那是新房子。
Nà shì xīn fáng zi.
That is a new house.
他是我的朋友。
Tā shì wǒ de péng you.
He is my friend.
是 means "is / am / are". Put it between two things.

你住在这里吗？
Nǐ zhù zài zhè lǐ ma?
Do you live here?
这是你的房子吗？
Zhè shì nǐ de fáng zi ma?
Is this your house?
你要搬家吗？
Nǐ yào bān jiā ma?
Are you moving house?
Add 吗 at the end to make a yes/no question.
dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

搬家了 Bān jiā le

A：你好！你住在哪里？
Nǐ hǎo! Nǐ zhù zài nǎ lǐ?
Hi! Where do you live?
B：我住在这里。
Wǒ zhù zài zhè lǐ.
I live here.
A：你的家大吗？
Nǐ de jiā dà ma?
Is your home big?
B：不大，很小。
Bù dà, hěn xiǎo.
Not big, very small.
A：这是你的新家吗？
Zhè shì nǐ de xīn jiā ma?
Is this your new home?
B：是，我搬家了。
Shì, wǒ bān jiā le.
Yes, I moved house.
