dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

你想吃什么？ Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?

A — Ask
你饿了吗？
Nǐ è le ma?
Are you hungry?
B — Answer
我饿了。我想吃面条。
Wǒ è le. Wǒ xiǎng chī miàntiáo.
I'm hungry. I want to eat noodles.
A — Ask
你好吗？
Nǐ hǎo ma?
How are you?
B — Answer
我很好，谢谢！
Wǒ hěn hǎo, xièxie!
I'm very good, thanks!
