G1 · 离
Grammar 1 · 语法1
A
place A
+
离
lí
+
B
place B
+
近 / 远
near / far
A is near / far from B
我家离超市很近。
Wǒ jiā lí chāo shì hěn jìn.
My home is very close to the supermarket.
他的家离学校很远。
Tā de jiā lí xué xiào hěn yuǎn.
His home is very far from school.
新公司离公园很近。
Xīn gōng sī lí gōng yuán hěn jìn.
The new company is very close to the park.
离 tells us the distance between two places.
G2 · 先…然后
Grammar 2 · 语法2
先
xiān
+
action 1
动作一
，
然后
rán hòu
+
action 2
动作二
first…, then…
我先吃饭，然后搬家。
Wǒ xiān chī fàn, rán hòu bān jiā.
First I eat, then I move house.
你先找到工作，然后找房子。
Nǐ xiān zhǎo dào gōng zuò, rán hòu zhǎo fáng zi.
First find a job, then look for a house.
搬家那天，我们先收拾东西，然后坐车去新家。
Bān jiā nà tiān, wǒ men xiān shōu shi dōng xi, rán hòu zuò chē qù xīn jiā.
On moving day, first pack things, then take a car to the new home.
Use 先…然后 to describe two actions in order.
G3 · 比 / 更

这里比那里安静。
Zhè lǐ bǐ nà lǐ ān jìng.
Here is quieter than there.
新房子比旧房子大。
Xīn fáng zi bǐ jiù fáng zi dà.
The new house is bigger than the old house.
住在城市更方便。
Zhù zài chéng shì gèng fāng biàn.
Living in the city is even more convenient.
比 compares two things. 更 adds emphasis: "even more".
dialogue
Dialogue · 对话

你为什么要搬家？ Nǐ wèi shén me yào bān jiā?

A：你为什么要搬家？
Nǐ wèi shén me yào bān jiā?
Why do you want to move house?
B：因为我的家离超市太远。
Yīn wèi wǒ de jiā lí chāo shì tài yuǎn.
Because my home is too far from the supermarket.
A：你想搬到什么地方？
Nǐ xiǎng bān dào shén me dì fāng?
Where do you want to move to?
B：我想搬到离公司近的地方。
Wǒ xiǎng bān dào lí gōng sī jìn de dì fāng.
I want to move somewhere close to the company.
A：你找到新房子了吗？
Nǐ zhǎo dào xīn fáng zi le ma?
Have you found a new house?
B：还没有。我先找工作，然后找房子。
Hái méi yǒu. Wǒ xiān zhǎo gōng zuò, rán hòu zhǎo fáng zi.
Not yet. First I’ll find a job, then look for a house.
