3. 国际合作进展汇报：国际学术会议筹备

现将本周国际合作方面的一项进展向二位领导汇报：我与葡萄牙埃武拉大学艺术学院院长 Tiago Navarro Marques 教授继续举行了会谈。双方正筹划与中国艾思科蓝公司（AiScholar，https://www.ais.cn）合作举办国际学术会议。承蒙葡方重视，邀请本人担任会议演讲嘉宾（speaker），这也是对我校学术影响力的一种认可。

3. Progress Report on International Cooperation: International Conference in Preparation

I would like to report to the directors a development in international cooperation this week: talks continued with Prof. Tiago Navarro Marques, Director of the School of Arts at Universidade de Évora (Portugal). Together, we are planning an international academic conference in partnership with the Chinese company AiScholar (https://www.ais.cn). The Évora side has kindly honoured me with an invitation to take a speaker role at the conference, which I also see as recognition of our university's academic standing.

Given the potential of this event, I would like to respectfully seek the directors' guidance: might it be appropriate for Hainan University to join as an official partner in co-organising this international conference? This could further strengthen our university's international cooperation and deepen the academic relationship between our two universities and between the Chinese and Portuguese institutions involved.

I would be grateful for the directors' consideration and instructions on whether this direction is appropriate. Should the directors find it suitable, I will follow up with Prof. Tiago and AiScholar on the concrete arrangements, in line with the directors' guidance.
