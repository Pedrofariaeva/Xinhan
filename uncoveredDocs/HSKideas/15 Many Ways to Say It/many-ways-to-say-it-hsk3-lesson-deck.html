concept
Core Idea · 核心概念

Same Goal, Different Words tóng yí gè yìsi, bùtóng de shuōfǎ

The Textbook 课本
我要一份炒拉面。
Wǒ yào yí fèn chǎo lā miàn.
One sentence. One pattern. Always works — in the book.
Real Life 真实生活
来一份炒拉面！ / 给我炒拉面！
麻烦你，炒拉面一份。/ 有炒拉面吗？
Same noodles. Six-plus different sentences.
Key skill: Listen for the keyword, not the wrapper. 炒拉面 / 哪国 / 怎么走 stays the same; everything around it changes.
scene 1
Scenario 1 · 场景一

点菜：炒拉面 diǎn cài: chǎo lā miàn

1
我要一份炒拉面。
Wǒ yào yí fèn chǎo lā miàn.
I want an order of fried noodles.
2
我想吃炒拉面。
Wǒ xiǎng chī chǎo lā miàn.
I'd like to eat fried noodles.
3
给我一份炒拉面。
Gěi wǒ yí fèn chǎo lā miàn.
Give me an order of fried noodles.
4
来一份炒拉面。
Lái yí fèn chǎo lā miàn.
One order of fried noodles, please. (casual)
scene 1
Scenario 1 · 场景一 (continued)

点菜：炒拉面 diǎn cài: chǎo lā miàn

5
有炒拉面吗？我要一份。
Yǒu chǎo lā miàn ma? Wǒ yào yí fèn.
Do you have fried noodles? I'll take one.
6
麻烦你，炒拉面一份。
Máfan nǐ, chǎo lā miàn yí fèn.
Sorry to trouble you — one fried noodles.
7
可以给我炒拉面吗？
Kěyǐ gěi wǒ chǎo lā miàn ma?
Could you give me fried noodles?
8
请问，有炒拉面吗？
Qǐngwèn, yǒu chǎo lā miàn ma?
Excuse me, do you have fried noodles?
Pattern: 我要 / 我想 / 给我 / 来 / 有…吗 / 麻烦你 / 可以…吗 / 请问 — these openers all work for ordering.
scene 2
Scenario 2 · 场景二

问原籍：你从哪儿来？ wèn yuánjí: nǐ cóng nǎr lái?

1
你是哪国人？
Nǐ shì nǎ guó rén?
What country are you from?
2
你从哪儿来？
Nǐ cóng nǎr lái?
Where do you come from?
3
你是哪里人？
Nǐ shì nǎ lǐ rén?
Where are you from? (which place)
4
你老家在哪儿？
Nǐ lǎojiā zài nǎr?
Where's your hometown?
5
你来自哪个国家？
Nǐ láizì nǎge guójiā?
Which country do you come from? (formal)
6
你是从哪个地方来的？
Nǐ shì cóng nǎge dìfang lái de?
Which place are you from?
scene 3
Scenario 3 · 场景三

问路：怎么去火车站？ wèn lù: zěnme qù huǒchēzhàn?

1
请问，去火车站怎么走？
Qǐngwèn, qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zǒu?
Excuse me, how do I get to the train station?
2
火车站在哪儿？
Huǒchēzhàn zài nǎr?
Where is the train station?
3
去火车站怎么坐车？
Qù huǒchēzhàn zěnme zuò chē?
How do I take a bus/car to the train station?
4
火车站离这儿远不远？
Huǒchēzhàn lí zhèr yuǎn bu yuǎn?
Is the train station far from here?
5
火车站离这儿有多远？
Huǒchēzhàn lí zhèr yǒu duō yuǎn?
How far is the train station from here?
6
怎么去火车站？
Zěnme qù huǒchēzhàn?
How do I go to the train station?
tips
Survival Tips · 生存技巧

Survive Any Phrasing rènghé shuōfǎ dōu néng tīng dǒng

1 · Catch the keyword

炒拉面 / 哪国 / 怎么走 stays the same. Ignore the wrapper, catch the noun.

2 · Learn the openers

我要 / 我想 / 给我 / 来 / 可以…吗 / 请问 — once you know these, most requests become readable.

3 · Ask back

你的意思是…吗？ (Do you mean...?) or 可以再说一次吗？ (Can you say that again?)

4 · Collect one scene per week

Pick ordering food, directions, or small talk. Write every phrasing you actually hear — not just the textbook one.
