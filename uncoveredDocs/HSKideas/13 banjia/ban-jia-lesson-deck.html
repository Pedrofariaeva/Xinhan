G1 · 比
Grammar 1 · 语法一
A
subject
+
比
bǐ
+
B
object
+
adjective
形容词
A is more [adj] than B
猫比狗小。
māo bǐ gǒu xiǎo.
Cats are smaller than dogs.
大城市的房子比小城市的房子贵。
dà chéng shì de fáng zi bǐ xiǎo chéng shì de fáng zi guì.
Houses in big cities are more expensive than in small cities.
深圳的工资比我住的地方高，工作机会也比这里多。
Shēnzhèn de gōngzī bǐ wǒ zhù de dìfāng gāo, gōngzuò jīhuì yě bǐ zhèlǐ duō.
Shenzhen salaries are higher, and there are more job opportunities too.
Key: adjective goes at the END — never say ❌ 比大 — say ✅ 比…大
大城市的房子bǐ xiǎo chéng shì guì — more expensive than small cities
小城市的房子bǐ dà chéng shì piányí — cheaper than big cities
G2 · 更
Grammar 2 · 语法二
更
gèng
+
adjective
形容词
even more [adj] · adds emphasis
冬天的时候，南方更暖和。
dōng tiān de shí hòu, nán fāng gèng nuǎn huo.
In winter, the south is even warmer.
住在城市，买东西更方便。
zhù zài chéng shì, mǎi dōng xi gèng fāng biàn.
Living in the city, shopping is even more convenient.
Complete using 更

贵guì

方便fāng biàn
Note: 比 compares two things. 更 says "even more" — it adds intensity, often without naming what you compare to.
北方冬天 — 冷běi fāng dōng tiān · cold north in winter
南方冬天 — 更暖和nán fāng · even warmer in winter
