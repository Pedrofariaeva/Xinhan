Dialogue 1 · 对话一
你要点什么？
Nǐ yào diǎn shén me?
What would you like to order?
服
Waiter · 服务员
你好！请问，你要点什么？
Nǐ hǎo! Qǐng wèn, nǐ yào diǎn shén me?
Hello! May I ask, what would you like to order?
我
Me · 我
我要一碗牛肉炒面。
Wǒ yào yī wǎn niú ròu chǎo miàn.
I'd like one bowl of beef fried noodles.
服
Waiter · 服务员
好的，稍等！
Hǎo de, shāo děng!
Alright, just a moment!
Choose your dish · 选一个
A
牛肉炒面
niú ròu chǎo miàn
beef fried noodles
B
牛肉汤面
niú ròu tāng miàn
beef noodle soup
C
牛肉炒饭
niú ròu chǎo fàn
beef fried rice
📝
Pattern: 我要 + [number] + [measure word] + [dish].   Try saying your choice aloud using 我要一碗…

Dialogue 2 · 对话二
热的还是冰的？
Rè de háishì bīng de?
Hot or iced?
服
Waiter · 服务员
您要热的、冰的，还是常温的？
Nín yào rè de, bīng de, háishì cháng wēn de?
Would you like it hot, iced, or at room temperature?
我
Me · 我
我要冰的咖啡，谢谢。
Wǒ yào bīng de kā fēi, xiè xiè.
I'd like an iced coffee, thank you.
Choose your temperature · 选一个
A
热的
rè de
hot
B
冰的
bīng de
iced / cold
C
常温的
cháng wēn de
room temperature
📝
Pattern: 我要 + [temperature] + 的 + [drink].   The particle 的 links the adjective to the noun.

服

Key word: 份 (fèn) is the measure word for portions/servings. 一份 = one serving.

Dialogue 4 · 对话四
什么杯子？

What size cup?
服
Waiter · 服务员
您要什么杯子？中杯、小杯，还是超大杯？
Nín yào shén me bēi zi? Zhōng bēi, xiǎo bēi, háishì chāo dà bēi?
What size cup? Medium, small, or jumbo?
我
Me · 我

Chāo dà bēi, xiè xiè!
Jumbo size, please!

Key word: 杯 (bēi) = cup as a measure word. 杯子 (bēi zi) = the object "cup". 一杯咖啡 = one cup of coffee.
