H_CN: 1. 支持我校唐畅教授参加国际公共财政学会（IIPF）第82届年会 / Support for Professor Tang, Hainan University, 82nd Annual Congress of the IIPF
PHOTOS2: iipf_banner.jpg | 图1：IIPF第82届年会会标（里斯本 ISEG，2026年8月24–26日） | Fig. 1: IIPF 82nd Annual Congress banner, ISEG Lisbon, 24–26 August 2026 || main_blurred.jpg | 图2：参会纪念（会议资料与胸牌） | Fig. 2: Congress participation — with conference materials and badge
P_CN: 8月24日至26日，我赴葡萄牙里斯本迎接并支持唐畅教授参加国际公共财政学会（IIPF）第82届年会。本届年会主题为“动荡中的公共财政”（Public Finances in Turmoil），由里斯本大学经济管理学院（ISEG）主办，UECE 与 ISEG Research 协办，学术委员会主席为 Naomi Feldman 教授与 Dirk Foremny 教授。8月24日的开幕式暨首场全体会议由 James Hines（密歇根大学）、Paulo Lopes Henriques、António Afonso（ISEG）与 Eckhard Janeba（曼海姆大学）等学者出席，围绕当前全球公共财政面临的挑战展开讨论。
P_EN: From 24 to 26 August I support Professor Tang to her welcoming for the 82nd Annual Congress of the International Institute of Public Finance (IIPF) in Lisbon, Portugal. The congress theme was "Public Finances in Turmoil", hosted by ISEG – Lisbon School of Economics and Management, University of Lisbon, co-organised with UECE and ISEG Research, with Prof. Naomi Feldman and Prof. Dirk Foremny as Scientific Chairs. The Opening Session on 24 August featured James Hines (University of Michigan), Paulo Lopes Henriques, António Afonso (ISEG) and Eckhard Janeba (University of Mannheim), discussing the current challenges facing public finances worldwide.

H_CN: 2. Claudito 工具升级 / Claudito Tool Upgrades
P_CN: Claudito 从 v2.7.30 迭代至 v2.7.60，共发布30个版本，本期重点是成员邀请与全自动接入流程：邀请邮件确认机制（发送确认码、可重新发送、过期邀请自动完成、管理员永不被降级）；新成员接入完全自动化——自动安装 Git、自动配置 SSH 密钥、不再要求 AWS 设置、成员无需拥有自己的账号即可下载团队项目；项目列表改用成员本人凭证获取，网络操作增加超时保护；邮件回复现在直接送达邀请人，不再进入无回复黑洞。这些改进确保了工具在九月各团队正式部署前的稳定性。
P_EN: Claudito evolved from v2.7.30 to v2.7.60 — 30 releases — with the focus on the member invitation and fully automatic onboarding pipeline: e-mail confirmation for invitations (confirmation codes, resend on request, stale invitations completed automatically, admins never demoted); member onboarding now fully automatic — git is installed automatically, the SSH key is set up automatically, members are no longer asked for AWS settings, and a member can download the team's projects without an account of their own; the team project list is now fetched with the member's own credential and network calls have real timeouts; mail replies now reach the inviter instead of a no-reply black hole. These improvements secure the tool's stability ahead of the teams' formal deployment in September.

H_CN: 3. 项目文献质量核查与更新 / Project References Quality Check and Update
P_CN: 老年人工智能友好住宅项目（Elderly AI-Friendly Housing Project）与动物福利（Animal Welfare）项目方面，本期按计划开展了参考文献的质量核查工作：对已检索到的文献逐一检查质量，与项目计划的要求进行匹配，剔除了不符合要求的文献，并更新了项目的文献库，确保项目资料库的准确性与完整性。
P_EN: For the Elderly AI-Friendly Housing and Animal Welfare projects, the scheduled quality check of the references was carried out this period: each retrieved paper was reviewed for quality and matched against the project plan requirements; papers that did not meet the criteria were removed and the project bibliography was updated, ensuring the accuracy and completeness of the project literature base.
