H_CN: 1. 动物福利研究进展（已与刘处沟通，按指导意见调整）/ Animal Welfare Research Progress (adjusted following discussion with Director Liu)
P_CN: 本周与刘处就动物福利研究方向进行了沟通，并根据指导意见对后续工作做出以下调整：
P_EN: This week I discussed the Animal Welfare research direction with Director Liu. Based on her guidance, the work will be adjusted as follows:
P_CN: **（1）扩展物种范围。**在原有犬、猫、马的基础上，将研究范围扩展至小鼠、兔、猴、反刍动物（牛、绵羊、山羊）、鸡、猪，形成更完整的物种比较矩阵。初步核查已完成：欧盟对上述全部物种均有规定；中国 GB 14925-2023 覆盖其中七种，牛、羊与马无规定；美国联邦法规仅覆盖四种，小鼠、鸡及食用或纤维用途的农场动物由法律明确排除。
P_EN: **(1) Expanding the species scope.** Beyond dogs, cats and horses, the research will cover the mouse, rabbit, monkey, ruminants (cattle, sheep and goats), chicken and pig, forming a more complete species comparison matrix. A first check is complete: the EU regulates all of them; the Chinese standard GB 14925-2023 covers seven of the ten, with nothing for cattle, sheep, goats or horses; and US federal regulation covers only four, since mice, chickens and farm animals used for food or fibre are excluded by the Act itself.
P_CN: **（2）强化环境与热带语境——以医学为导向。**热带方向的重点从农业视角转向医学与生物安全视角，包括人畜共患病、媒介传播疾病、真菌与细菌滋生，以及湿度与温差对建筑环境的影响。核查中的一个重要发现是：GB 14925-2023 的三类环境（普通、屏障、隔离）并非舒适度等级，而是按动物的微生物等级划分——该环境标准实质上是一项生物安全标准。而四个体系均未按气候分区作出规定，湿热地区的相关要求既无规定性数值也无性能化指引，这正是我们的研究空白，也是学院国际化的重要机会。
P_EN: **(2) Strengthening the environment and the tropical context — with a medical focus.** The tropical dimension shifts from an agricultural framing to a medical and biosecurity one: zoonotic transmission, vector-borne disease, fungal and bacterial proliferation, and the effect of humidity and temperature variation on the built environment. A key finding: the three environment classes of GB 14925-2023 — conventional, barrier and isolation — are not comfort categories but are defined by the microbiological status of the animals, which makes the environmental standard a biosecurity standard in substance. None of the four systems regulates by climate zone, so hot and humid regions have neither prescriptive figures nor performance guidance. That is our research gap, and a significant opportunity for the School's international positioning.
P_CN: **（3）建立更清晰的数据表格。**已按新方向整理出五张核查对比表：物种覆盖、最小空间要求、环境参数、热带医学风险矩阵、规定性与性能化标准对比。表中数值均取自各国法规与标准原文，标准未作规定之处如实注明，不以二手资料代替。
P_EN: **(3) Creating clearer, more perceptible tables.** Five comparison tables have been prepared under the new direction: species coverage, minimum space requirements, environmental parameters, tropical-medical risk matrix, and prescriptive versus performance-based regulation. Every figure is taken from the laws and standards themselves; where an instrument is silent the table says so, and no secondary source is used in their place.
P_CN: 下一步：完成各物种在欧盟、美国与中国标准下的全部数值并定稿表格，撰写“环境即疾病防控”章节，扩展结论中的热带部分；同时补充两份尚缺的标准原文——GB 14922《实验动物 微生物、寄生虫学等级及监测》，以及适用于牛、羊的中国畜禽福利或养殖环境标准。
P_EN: Next steps: complete the EU, US and Chinese values for every species and finalise the tables; write the chapter on environment as disease control; extend the tropical part of the conclusion; and obtain the two standards still missing — GB 14922 on the microbiological grades of laboratory animals, and a Chinese livestock welfare or farm-environment standard for cattle, sheep and goats.
