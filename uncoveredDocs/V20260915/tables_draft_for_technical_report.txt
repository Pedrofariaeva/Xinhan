H_CN: 详细内容 / Details

H_CN: 1. 动物福利技术框架：已完成的工作 / Animal Welfare Technical Framework: Work Completed
P_CN: 文献方面，参考文献由原来的数十篇精简为32篇，每一篇均取得原文并核实其类型：7部法律法规、2项标准、4份指南或机构文件、17篇同行评审论文，以及1篇书籍章节和1篇技术文章。凡是规定性数值，只引用法律法规或标准原文；论文只用于说明“为什么”，并注明作者所在国家。核查中还发现，一份被广泛引用的英国机构对比文件（NC3Rs，2024）存在数据错位，例如欧盟猫的面积数据被错列；本项目的所有数值均直接取自标准原文，未采用该二手资料。
P_EN: On the literature, the reference list was reduced to 32 sources, each held as the original document and classified by type: 7 legal instruments, 2 standards, 4 guidance or organisational documents, 17 peer-reviewed papers, and one book chapter and one technical article. Numerical requirements are quoted only from laws or standards; papers are used only to explain why a requirement matters, with the country of the main authors stated. The check also found that a widely used UK comparison document (NC3Rs, 2024) contains transposed data — for example, the EU figures for cats are shifted between columns — so every figure in this project is taken directly from the instruments, not from that secondary source.
P_CN: 对比结论方面，美国与中国的最小空间要求彼此接近，均明显低于欧盟与英国；但更关键的差异不在面积，而在于各体系在哪些方面做出数值规定。据此提出“按需定尺”（size to fit）的设计原则：围栏尺寸应从动物本身及其必要行为出发确定，再对照建筑必须维持的环境条件进行校核。
P_EN: On the comparison, the American and Chinese minimum space requirements are close to each other and both well below the European and British ones; but the more important difference is not the area — it is where each system chooses to set numbers. From this the report proposes the design principle of “size to fit”: an enclosure is dimensioned from the animal and what it must be able to do, and then checked against the environmental conditions the building has to hold.

H_CN: 2. 审阅意见与调整方向 / Review Guidance and Adjustments
P_CN: **（1）扩展物种范围。**在原有犬、猫、马的基础上，将研究范围扩展至小鼠、兔、猴、反刍动物（牛、绵羊、山羊）、鸡和猪，形成完整的物种比较矩阵（表1、表2）。
P_EN: **(1) Expanding the species scope.** Beyond dogs, cats and horses, the research now covers the mouse, rabbit, monkey, ruminants (cattle, sheep and goats), chicken and pig, forming a complete species comparison matrix (Tables 1 and 2).
P_CN: **（2）强化环境与热带语境——以医学为导向。**热带方向的重点从农业视角转向医学与生物安全视角。一个重要发现是：中国标准 GB 14925-2023 的三类环境（普通环境、屏障环境、隔离环境）并非舒适度等级，而是按动物的微生物等级划分的——其环境标准本质上是一项生物安全标准。在湿热气候下，维持屏障环境所依赖的机械系统（过滤送风、压差梯度、温湿度控制）负荷更大，一旦失效后果也更严重。而四个体系均未按气候分区做出规定（表3、表4）。
P_EN: **(2) Strengthening the environment and tropical context — with a medical focus.** The tropical dimension shifts from an agricultural framing to a medical and biosecurity one. A key finding: the three environment classes of the Chinese standard GB 14925-2023 — conventional, barrier and isolation — are not comfort categories but are defined by the microbiological status of the animals; the environmental standard is, in substance, a biosecurity standard. In a hot and humid climate, the mechanical systems that hold a barrier environment — filtered supply air, pressure cascade, temperature and humidity control — work harder and fail more consequentially. None of the four systems regulates by climate zone (Tables 3 and 4).
P_CN: **（3）建立更清晰的数据表格。**后续统一采用五类核心表格：物种覆盖表、物种空间要求对比表、环境参数对比表、热带医学风险矩阵、规定性与性能化标准对比表。表中所有数值均取自标准原文；标准未作规定之处，表中如实注明“无规定”，不以其他来源代替。
P_EN: **(3) Creating clearer, more perceptible tables.** All comparisons are standardised into five core tables: species coverage, space requirements by species, environmental parameters, tropical-medical risk matrix, and prescriptive versus performance-based regulation. Every figure is taken from the instrument itself; where an instrument is silent, the table says “no rule” instead of borrowing a figure from elsewhere.
